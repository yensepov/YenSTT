# Code of conduct

YenSTT contributors and users are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through https://github.com/yensepov. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where practical.
